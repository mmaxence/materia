    Chrome 156 on macOS; 1280 × 720 viewport, DPR 2, 800 × 240 text host. Accordion, Dither and RGB Split; 30 warm-up frames, 120 sampled intervals, five mounts and 30 updates per configuration. No intervals exceeded 25 ms in this run. Update calls were ≤ 0.1 ms at p95.

    Frame intervals measure requestAnimationFrame scheduling, not GPU execution. Update latency measures a settings call to the next animation frame, not input-to-photon latency. These are local desktop observations, not mobile or production-network guarantees.

    The isolated benchmark loaded in 47.7 ms (DOMContentLoaded: 46.9 ms) over localhost with browser caching enabled. This is a development baseline, not a cold-download estimate. Bundle sizes use esbuild minification and gzip; they exclude fonts, imagery and showcase code. kB = 1,000 bytes.
